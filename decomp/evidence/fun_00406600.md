## Early exits and reconstruction choice

The original 27-byte machine-code body contains no conditional branch and no native early-return path: it always forwards the fixed six arguments to `FUN_004061F0`, performs `add esp,0x18`, and returns.

The portable host reconstruction adds only two harness-safety exits: missing services and missing dispatch callback. Both are explicitly tested and are not claimed as original binary control flow. The implementation treats `FUN_004061F0` as an opaque injected service and does not modify or reimplement parallel assignments `FUN_004065A0`, `FUN_004065D0`, or `FUN_00406620`.
