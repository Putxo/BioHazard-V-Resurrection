## Callee relationship

`FUN_00407A90` is already recovered and validated as the 32-bit aligned
allocation adapter with ABI:

~~~text
void* FUN_00407A90(uint32_t requested_size, const void* unused_type_tag)
~~~

Its target body ignores the second semantic argument, replaces that ABI slot
with fixed alignment `0x10`, and forwards to the underlying allocator. The
portable recovery exposes two host-only safety exits when the allocator
service or callback is unavailable.

E70 performs exactly:

1. read its sole stack argument;
2. push literal zero as A90 argument 2;
3. push the original size as A90 argument 1;
4. call `FUN_00407A90`;
5. clean the two cdecl arguments;
6. return the A90 pointer in EAX unchanged;
7. pop its own single stack argument with `ret 4`.

No size adjustment, object-field access, post-call transformation, or
E70-specific guard exists.
