## Early exits and reconstruction choice

The original 27-byte body has no conditional branch and no early-return path: it always forwards the six constants to `FUN_004061F0`, cleans the stack, and returns.

The portable reconstruction adds only two harness guards: a null-services guard and a null-dispatch guard. Both are unit-tested explicitly as host-only early exits and are not claimed as original binary control flow. The normal-path test verifies every observed forwarded argument.

`FUN_00406680`, `FUN_004066C0`, and `FUN_004066E0` are active or parallel assignments and are not modified by this recovery. `FUN_004061F0` is treated as an existing opaque dispatch dependency; this candidate only reconstructs the fixed forwarding wrapper at `0x00406700`.
