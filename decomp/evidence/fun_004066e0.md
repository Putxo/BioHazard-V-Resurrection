## Early exits and reconstruction choice

The original 27-byte machine-code body contains no conditional branch and no native early-return path: it always forwards the fixed six arguments to `FUN_004061F0`, performs `add esp,0x18`, and returns.

The portable host reconstruction adds only two harness-safety exits: missing services and missing dispatch callback. Both are explicitly unit-tested and are not claimed as original binary control flow. The normal-path test verifies every forwarded constant.

`FUN_00406680`, `FUN_004066C0`, and `FUN_00406700` are active or parallel assignments and are not modified by this recovery. `FUN_004061F0` remains an existing opaque dispatch dependency; this candidate reconstructs only `FUN_004066E0`.
