## Early exits and reconstruction choice

The original 27-byte body has no conditional branch and no early-return path: it always forwards the six constants to `FUN_004061F0`, cleans the stack, and returns.

The portable reconstruction adds only two harness-safety exits: missing services and missing dispatch callback. Both are explicitly unit-tested and are not claimed as original binary control flow. The normal-path test verifies every forwarded constant.

`FUN_004067C0`, `FUN_004067E0`, and `FUN_00406820` are parallel/assigned work and are not modified by this recovery. `FUN_004061F0` is treated only as an existing opaque dispatch dependency; this candidate reconstructs the fixed forwarding wrapper at `0x00406800`.
